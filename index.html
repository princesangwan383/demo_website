About CHIC

Crafted by Certified Hands. Inspired by You.

At CHIC Unisex Salon & Academy, beauty is more than a service — it's a discipline. Every member of our team is a certified stylist trained at our in-house academy, blending global techniques with an intuitive understanding of Indian hair, skin, and personal expression.

From the moment you walk in, expect a calm, inclusive space designed for everyone. Whether you're here for a sharp new cut, a glowing facial, statement nail art, or your bridal day — we deliver precision, hygiene, and a touch of artistry in every session.
